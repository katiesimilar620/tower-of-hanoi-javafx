## 🎮 What Is This Game?

Tower of Hanoi is a classic brain teaser that has challenged players for over a century. You'll see three vertical rods and a set of colorful rings stacked on the leftmost rod. The rings are different sizes, with the largest at the bottom and the smallest at the top.

**Your goal:** Move all rings from the first rod to the third rod. Sounds easy, right? Here's the catch:
- You can only move **one ring at a time**
- You can never place a larger ring on top of a smaller ring
- You have a spare rod to help you reorganize

It sounds simple, but solving it requires careful planning and logical thinking. The game gets harder as you add more rings!

## 🎯 How to Play

### The Basics

1. **Click** on any ring you want to move
2. **Click** on the rod where you want to place it
3. The ring will automatically move if the move is legal

### Winning the Game

To win, all rings must end up on the third rod (the rightmost one). The game will cheer you on with a victory screen when you complete the puzzle. You'll see your star rating and the number of moves you used.

## 📚 Understanding the Logic

The Tower of Hanoi puzzle has a beautiful mathematical pattern. The minimum number of moves needed is **2^n - 1**, where n is the number of rings. So:
- 3 rings = 7 moves minimum
- 4 rings = 15 moves
- 5 rings = 31 moves
- 6 rings = 63 moves

This exponential growth is why the puzzle gets so much harder with more rings!
